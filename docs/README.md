# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This documentation set provides a practical, end-to-end view of how OctoAcme organizes project work from concept through release and learning. It is designed to help teams align on outcomes, deliver iteratively, and maintain clear ownership across planning, execution, communication, and quality assurance.

## Project management process overview

OctoAcme begins with initiation, where teams validate the business need, define stakeholder alignment, confirm success metrics, and decide whether work should move forward. Once approved, the project enters planning, where deliverables are broken into a prioritized backlog, milestones are mapped, dependencies are identified, and a Definition of Done is documented. This creates a shared understanding of scope, effort, and accountability before implementation starts.

During execution and tracking, delivery work is managed through a project board, regular standups, and a structured pull request workflow. Teams focus on small, reviewable changes, link work to issues, maintain QA and CI checks, and escalate blockers through clear business and technical paths. Reporting and metrics help monitor delivery health and the progress toward identified project goals, while the team uses demos and weekly reviews to keep stakeholders informed.

Risk management and communication are treated as ongoing responsibilities rather than separate admin tasks. OctoAcme maintains a risk register, identifies stakeholders and communication needs, and shares regular status updates through weekly and milestone-based reporting. When issues escalate, the process defines a clear chain from team-level triage to project and sponsor-level intervention, with incident communication templates and post-incident follow-through used to maintain trust and learning.

Release and deployment follow a disciplined checklist to reduce change risk and improve observability. Teams verify acceptance criteria, run CI and security checks, test in staging, prepare rollback plans, and communicate deployment outcomes to stakeholders and support. At the end of each sprint, release, or major milestone, the team holds a retrospective to capture what went well, what can improve, and which action items need follow-up. This continuous improvement loop helps OctoAcme refine its process over time and make gradual, measurable improvements to delivery quality.

## Roles and personas

OctoAcme relies on a small set of core roles that work together across the lifecycle. Product managers define outcomes and prioritize the roadmap, project managers coordinate planning, communication, risks, and scheduling, and developers build, test, and deliver software. QA/testing validates quality and readiness, while stakeholders provide input, approval, and business context. Clear role ownership supports accountability while encouraging collaboration across disciplines.

## How to use this set

Use these documents as a single source of guidance for OctoAcme project work. Start with the overview and initiation guide when a new idea is proposed, move into planning as work becomes actionable, and consult execution, communication, release, and retrospective guidance throughout delivery. This structure keeps project activities consistent, transparent, and easier to onboard into across the team.
